## 这次轮到父母说等一会儿

原PDF第18页；04:09—04:53

When the children came closer, Mum and Dad turned around. They wore a cardboard TV on their heads. "In a bit," said Mum and Dad.

What's the meaning of "In a bit"? That means "Not right now. You have to wait." You kids have to wait for your dinner.

“等一会儿”是什么意思？意思是“不是现在。你得等等。”孩子们，你们得等一等才能吃晚饭。

When Mum and Dad needed help, the children said, "In a bit." When the children wanted dinner, Mum and Dad also said, "In a bit."

妈妈和爸爸需要帮忙时，孩子们说：“等一会儿。”孩子们想吃晚饭时，妈妈和爸爸也说：“等一会儿。”
