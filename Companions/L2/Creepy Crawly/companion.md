## 封面：秘密盒子里的小爬虫

原PDF第1页；00:00—01:16

Hi there! Story time! Today's story is called "Creepy-crawly!" Do you know what a creepy-crawly is? Let's break it down!

嗨！讲故事的时间到啦！今天的故事叫《小爬虫！》。你知道什么是“creepy-crawly”吗？我们把这个词拆开看看吧！

"Creepy" means scary or unsettling, like a ghost. "Crawl" means to move on hands and knees, like a baby.

“creepy”是吓人或让人不安的意思，就像鬼一样。“crawl”是用手和膝盖爬行的意思，就像小宝宝那样。

Do you like crawling? A creepy-crawly is a small crawling creature. Can you guess what? Spiders, centipedes, or worms!

你喜欢爬来爬去吗？“creepy-crawly”就是爬来爬去的小生物。你能猜到有哪些吗？蜘蛛、蜈蚣，还有蚯蚓！

Look at the cover. Wilma had a secret box from the joke shop. She and Wilf looked excited.

看看封面。Wilma有一个从恶作剧玩具店买来的神秘盒子。她和Wilf看起来很兴奋。

Why? What was inside the box? A creepy-crawly? Let's find out!

为什么呢？盒子里是什么？一只小爬虫吗？我们来看看吧！
