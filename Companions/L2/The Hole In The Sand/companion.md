## 海水进来了

原PDF第12、13页；02:14—02:37

After a while, the water came in. All the children got out of the hole. Dad got in and tried to scoop the water out with his big spade.

过了一会儿，海水涌了进来。孩子们全都出了沙坑。爸爸走进坑里，想用大铲子把水舀出去。

But more and more water came in. Could Dad stop the water? No, he couldn't. "I can't stop it," said Dad.

可是涌进来的水越来越多。爸爸能挡住海水吗？不能。“我挡不住它。”爸爸说。
