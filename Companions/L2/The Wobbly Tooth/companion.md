## 封面：摇晃的牙和牙仙子

原PDF第1页；00:00—00:44

Hi there! Story time. Today's story is called "The Wobbly Tooth". What's the meaning of "wobbly"?

嗨！讲故事的时间到啦。今天的故事叫《松动的牙齿》。“wobbly”是什么意思呢？

Look at this jelly. It is wobbly. Look at me! I am standing on one foot. I am wobbly.

看看这个果冻，它摇摇晃晃的。看看我！我正单脚站着。我也摇摇晃晃的。

A wobbly tooth means the tooth is loose. It will come out.

“wobbly tooth”指的是松动的牙齿。它就快掉下来了。

What will happen when a loose tooth comes out? A tooth fairy will come. She will take away the tooth and leave a coin behind.

松动的牙齿掉下来后，会发生什么呢？牙仙子会来。她会拿走牙齿，留下一枚硬币。

One wobbly tooth equals one coin. Do you have any wobbly teeth?

一颗松动的牙可以换一枚硬币。你有松动的牙齿吗？
